## Key Notes
- PET/fMRI shows prefrontal cortex deactivation and nucleus accumbens/PAG activation at orgasm []
- Prolactin increases 300% post-orgasm, higher after intercourse than masturbation []
- Clitoral orgasms via pudendal nerve; vaginal via pelvic/hypogastric nerves []
- Byproduct hypothesis and pair-bonding hypothesis most evidence-supported []
- FSFI and ORS validated; vaginal photoplethysmography gold standard []
- 10-15% prevalence; directed masturbation 80-90% effective; SSRIs leading iatrogenic cause []

## Open Questions
- No large-sample (n>500) neuroimaging studies of orgasm
- Underrepresentation of non-Western populations
- No longitudinal menopause transition studies
- No validated wearable for continuous measurement

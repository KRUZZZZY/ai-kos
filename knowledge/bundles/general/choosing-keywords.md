Good keywords make AI-KOS work. They determine which articles get linked and how discoverable content is. A keyword strategy has three goals: (1) link to closely related articles by sharing keywords, (2) avoid false links to unrelated articles, (3) make search results relevant. Always include the main topic as keyword #1. Then include 1-2 broader categories the topic belongs to. Add the article type as a keyword so same-type articles can cluster. Include specific tools, technologies, or frameworks mentioned. Avoid: single-use words that no other article will use, overly generic terms like 'system' or 'tool', and keywords longer than 3 words. The sweet spot is 5-6 keywords per article. Three keywords is the minimum for linking to work. Eight is the maximum — more creates noise. After creating an article, run the linker and check what got connected. If an expected link is missing, check whether you share enough keywords with the target.

Since AI-KOS articles use [[wikilinks]] and are stored as .md files, the knowledge/ directory is already a valid Obsidian vault. Opening it in Obsidian would give us a graph view of all connected articles for free. The auto-linker ensures links stay fresh. This would give a visual way to explore the knowledge graph without building a custom UI. Could be useful for discovering unexpected connections between articles.
